Design System Analysis of Mobbin

The same monochrome, gallery-quiet system inverted onto a near-black canvas. White ink on deep neutral surfaces, stadium-pill controls that flip to white for primary actions, a ladder of low-lift dark tints instead of shadows, and Saans at unusual variable weights. The electric blue accent survives unchanged as the only chromatic signal.

02 — Foundations

Typography

Saans — a contemporary neo-grotesque used exclusively (Inter shown as substitute). The voice comes from unusual variable-font weight positions: a chunky 652 for every heading, a bookish 456 for text, an airy 300 for hero subtitles. Zero letter-spacing at every size; headlines end with a period.

09 — Foundations

Elevation & Depth

Level 0 — flat near-black canvas. Most of every page.
Level 1 — soft-canvas fill, no border. Nav pill, featured card, FAQ rows, footer.
Level 2 — 1px soft hairline on canvas. Pricing and testimonial cards.
Raised — the brightest neutral fill. Active toggle pills. No drop shadows anywhere.

Touch targets: the fixed-height CTA pills and toggle segments clear 44×44 WCAG-AAA; inputs pad to a similar height. Collapsing: the floating nav pill persists across breakpoints, tightening to logomark + CTA; pricing 2-up, portrait 3-up, and testimonial 4-up grids collapse column-by-column; section padding compresses from section-lg (120px) toward section (80px); display type scales down while keeping weight 652.
