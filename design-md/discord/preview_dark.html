Touch targets: button-primary and button-green clear 44x44 WCAG-AAA via their vertical padding. Collapsing: nav collapses to logo plus hamburger below the tablet breakpoint (Download CTA stays); multi-column pricing/feature grids reflow to a single stacked column; section padding compresses toward 20-32px; gradient cards and bands span full width; display type scales down while keeping weight 700-800.
