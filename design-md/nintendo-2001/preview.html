Design System Analysis of Nintendo.com (2001)

The web rendered as console hardware. Every region is a beveled periwinkle metal plate, edge-lit on top and shadow-lined below — the page reads as one machine faceplate. A carbon-navy command layer carries the navigation, the right rail, and the footer; warm amber and orange are rationed strictly as wayfinding signal. Outlined box-art wordmarks sit on full-bleed photographic hero fields.

02 — Foundations

Typography

Arial / Helvetica throughout — the era had no webfonts. Character comes from treatment, not typeface: every chrome label is uppercase Arial Bold with half-pixel tracking (silkscreen-legend voice), while hero wordmarks are heavy outlined-and-shadowed display type mimicking console box-art logotype. Body stays small and quiet at 12px.

08 — Foundations

Elevation & Depth

Inset / Plate — recessed bevel: darker chrome-indigo top edge, lighter bottom edge. List rows, fields, content plates.
Command slab — carbon near-black with halftone dot-matrix texture, sitting "above" the chrome. Nav bar, right-rail buttons, footer.

Design System Analysis of Nintendo.com (2001)

The dark-adapted Nintendo.com 2001 system — the console interface taken down to a midnight-navy machine under low light. Beveled metal plates still assemble the page, but the chassis darkens: the canvas drops to deep midnight navy, plates become dark slate-blue, and the bevel line lifts brighter than the surface so each panel's edge still catches light. Warm amber and orange wayfinding signal and outlined box-art wordmarks are preserved.
